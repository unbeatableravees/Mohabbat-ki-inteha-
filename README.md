# 📖 Mohabbat Ki Inteha  

*"A journey of love, trust, suspense, and healing through childhood memories, past traumas, and the ultimate bond of Zimal and Azaan."*  

---

## 🌹 About the Novel  

**Mohabbat Ki Inteha** is a romantic-suspense novel written in **Roman Urdu**, blending emotional depth with subtle intimacy and thrilling twists.  

- 🧡 *Romance:* Azaan and Zimal’s journey of trust, love, and bonding.  
- 🎭 *Emotional Depth:* Childhood traumas and flashbacks shaping their present.  
- 🔥 *Suspense:* Unexpected twists, thrilling turns, and emotional challenges.  
- 🌌 *Resolution:* Healing, closure, and the ultimate romantic finale.  

> Total Word Count: ~217,000+  
> Chapters: 30  
> Parts: 100+
